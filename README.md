# LinkedInHunter

LinkedInHunter
A Windows app that searches LinkedIn for jobs and hiring posts, then exports them to Excel.

**Download and install**
Go to https://github.com/ahaseeb1999/LinkedInHunter/releases/latest
Download LinkedInHunter-Setup-X.X.X.exe
Double-click to install. If Windows shows a warning ("publisher not recognized"), click More info → Run anyway.
Open the app from your desktop or Start menu.

First time setup
**When the app opens you have two options:**
Enter a license key: paste the key sent to you, click Activate.
Start 3-day free trial: no key needed, full access for 3 days.
_Need a key? Contact me (links at the bottom)._

**Add your LinkedIn account**
Click the Accounts tab in the sidebar.
Click Add Account.
A browser window opens. Sign in to LinkedIn the normal way.
After login it closes automatically. Your session is saved on your PC.
You can add more than one account if you want. This is recommended (see the caution section below).

**Run a search**
Click New Hunt.
Type one to three keywords (e.g. "React Native developer").
Pick what to search:
Jobs: job postings only
Posts: hiring posts from people
Both: both at once
Pick work mode: Remote / Remote+Hybrid / Any.
Click Start Hunt.
A browser window opens and works through results. You can Pause, Resume, or Stop anytime.

Tip: click Analyze My Profile to auto-fill keywords based on your LinkedIn profile.

See your results
The Results tab shows everything found.
Sort by date, title, or applicants.
Set status per row (New, Applied, Interviewing, Rejected, Offer).
Add notes per job.
Click the star to bookmark.
Export to Excel
In Results or Saved, click Export Excel.
File saves to your Downloads folder.
Two sheets: Jobs and Posts. Links are clickable.
Dashboard
The Dashboard tab shows totals, top companies, and status counts.
The Saved Jobs tab shows your bookmarks in a kanban board.

Common problems
"License revoked" or "Trial expired"
Contact me for a new key.

Your LinkedIn session may have expired. Go to Accounts → Check Session.
If still 0, contact me.
"Database is locked"
Close the app from Task Manager and reopen it. The app fixes the lock on next start.

"Failed to fetch" or server error
Check your internet. The app works offline for 24 hours after the last successful check.

Login window opens but the app doesn't recognize my sign-in
Wait, the app gives you up to 5 minutes. If LinkedIn shows extra steps (2FA, "save login info"), finish them. The app then continues automatically.

A note on LinkedIn (please read carefully)
This app uses your real LinkedIn account through a browser. LinkedIn does not allow automated tools in their terms of service. If you overuse the tool, LinkedIn can throttle your searches, block your IP, restrict your account, or in the worst case ban it.

**Use it carefully:**
Use a secondary LinkedIn account, not your main one.
The app lets you add multiple accounts. Make a separate LinkedIn account (with a different email) and use that one for hunting. Keep your main account safe.

Don't run hunts back to back.
After a hunt finishes, wait at least 15 to 60 minutes before starting another. Give the account a break. Treat it like a human, not a script.

Keep "Max Results" low.
25 to 100 results per hunt is safe. Don't push it to 200 or more every time.

Spread the load across accounts.
If you have 2 or 3 LinkedIn accounts added, switch between them between hunts. No single account should carry all the activity.

Don't run it all day.
A few hunts per hour is fine. Several per each time is not. Random short bursts look more like a real person browsing.

If LinkedIn shows a security check or asks you to verify, stop using the tool for that day.
Log into LinkedIn in your normal browser, complete whatever it asks, and wait 24 hours before using the tool again on that account.

You take full responsibility for how you use it. The tool is meant for personal job hunting, not bulk data collection or commercial scraping.
